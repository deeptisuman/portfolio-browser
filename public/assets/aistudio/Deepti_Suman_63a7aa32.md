- **Highlights:**

  - ~29 wireframe screens rendered in React with a consistent warm design system.
  - Multi-page PDF report (Python/ReportLab) covering a five-agent core architecture (Scout, Auditor, Concierge, Supervisor, Executor), MVP prioritization with **RICE scoring**, and a layered testing strategy.
  - MBA-level deliverables: alternatives comparison, 7Ms marketing framework, customer service strategy.
- **Skills shown:** Product strategy, prioritization, agentic AI architecture, PRD writing, wireframing, cost-aware LLM routing
